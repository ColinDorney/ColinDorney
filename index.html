Professional Background

Professional Profile

I am a Deaf federal civilian professional from El Paso, Texas, with experience in operational coordination, production tracking, employee training, technical manufacturing, and private-sector IT and security systems work. I earned my bachelor’s degree in Organizational Management & Leadership, graduating Summa Cum Laude with a 4.0 GPA.

My experience has taught me the value of precision, responsibility, clear communication, and working toward a common goal. I enjoy identifying problems, considering practical solutions, and taking initiative to build or improve something useful.

I am working toward a career in administration, office support, or operations coordination, combining my technical foundation with my education in organizational leadership.

Communication & Accessibility

As a Deaf professional, I communicate effectively with hearing colleagues through a combination of written communication, live captioning, transcription technology, and interpreting services when appropriate. I adapt to the situation and focus on clear, practical communication so I can collaborate, solve problems, and contribute fully in hearing-led workplaces.

Professional Goals

My immediate focus is to contribute in an administrative, office support, or operations coordination position where I can apply my leadership education, technical background, and problem-solving approach. I want to support a capable team, build stronger operational skills, and earn increasing responsibility through results.

Over time, I hope to grow into roles with broader coordination, improvement, and leadership responsibilities. My entrepreneurial projects also give me an outlet to develop practical ideas and turn them into tangible work.
