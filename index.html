A little about me

More than a job title.

I'm a curious, down-to-earth guy who enjoys working with others, figuring things out, and building ideas into something real. I have a background in CNC machining, and I'm building The Silent Gentleman as a business focused on education and challenging stereotypes about what Deaf people can accomplish.

Some of my favorite memories come from teamwork: CrossFit competitions, Boy Scouts, and my Eagle Scout service project improving a playground for a children's home—with volunteers, including my dad.

Building The Silent Gentleman

Through The Silent Gentleman, I want to educate people and challenge stereotypes about Deaf abilities. I’m developing a workplace training course focused on Deaf accessibility, communication, and the contributions Deaf professionals can make. The goal is to help employers see skills and potential—not just hearing status.

A family tradition of service

I have always wanted to serve in the U.S. military as a warfighter and continue my family’s tradition of military service. Being Deaf meant that path was not open to me, but my desire to serve never went away. I found another way to contribute through civilian service at Corpus Christi Army Depot—supporting the mission and carrying that tradition forward in my own way.
