A little about me

More than a job title.

I'm Colin, a Deaf Texan raised in El Paso in a family with military and business backgrounds. I was homeschooled, became an Eagle Scout, and grew up believing that character, responsibility, and keeping your word matter.

I once hoped to serve in the military or emergency services. Being Deaf closed those particular paths, so I built another through CNC machining and civilian service supporting the mission at Corpus Christi Army Depot. Along the way, I earned a bachelor's degree in Organizational Management and Leadership, graduating summa cum laude with a 4.0 GPA.

My experience goes beyond machining. I've worked in private-sector IT support, led a crew, and learned to work with people from different backgrounds. I enjoy solving problems, organizing work, taking initiative, and helping a team succeed.

Where I'm headed

I'm looking to bring that experience into administration, office support, operations coordination, or another people-focused role. I'm interested in work where clear communication, reliability, organization, and leadership matter.

My Eagle Scout project

One of my proudest accomplishments was helping improve a playground for a children's home. Planning the work, bringing volunteers together, and working alongside my dad taught me as much about leadership and service as any classroom.

Building something of my own

I'm developing The Silent Gentleman, a personal brand built around character, practical knowledge, humor, books, and education. I share ASL videos with captions and hope to develop workplace training and other useful resources over time.

Beyond the résumé

I'm usually quiet when I first meet someone, but I enjoy making people laugh, good-natured teasing, meaningful conversations, and adventure. My sign name comes from “Bold.” I value kindness, humility, integrity, and respect—old-fashioned principles that still belong in a modern world.

My Christian faith is part of my life, and so are travel, history, reading, fitness, and spending time with my dog, Dixie. I hope to build a family of my own someday and keep learning along the way.
